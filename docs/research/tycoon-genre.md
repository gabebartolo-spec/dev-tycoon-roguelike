# Research: the game-dev tycoon genre

Generated by a deep-research run (fan-out search, 3-vote adversarial verification). Question:

> The game-development tycoon sub-genre (Game Dev Tycoon, Mad Games Tycoon 1 and 2, Game Dev Story, Software Inc., Startup Company, Indie Dev Story and others): core loops, how projects are built (topic/genre combos, sliders, phases), how review scores, hype and bugs are computed (community-documented formulas), staff systems (stats, traits, training, burnout), economy and market lifecycles, era progression, what players praise and criticise, and the genre's known weaknesses (solved formulas, late-game snowballing, repetitiveness, opaque scoring). The goal is design input for a roguelike game-dev tycoon where cash is health, the procedurally generated market is the dungeon and bankruptcy is permadeath.

## Summary

The surviving evidence covers Game Dev Tycoon (GDT) almost entirely, plus a few roguelike-tycoon hybrids used as precedents. In GDT, each project starts with a topic, genre and platform (later also engine and audience). Development then runs through three stages, each with three focus sliders. Each slider feeds hidden design and tech ratios, and those add up to two totals, Design and Technology. GDT has no gameplay, graphics or sound score categories. The review score measures that quality against the player's own previous best, so every hit raises the bar. The game never explains this. That opacity is the best-documented weakness: reviews do not mention the player's choices, the score reveal feels random, and the best strategy means keeping external notes. Roguelike hybrids already exist (GOD CANDLE: 'the market is the dungeon'; Gamestonk Simulator's permadeath Survival mode with meta-currency; Goblin Vyke's dungeon-by-night, shop-by-day loop), but none found is a game-dev tycoon. For a design where cash is health, the main lessons are to make the scoring readable and to treat the rising self-relative bar as a ready-made difficulty ramp.

## Verified findings

### GDT project setup and core loop: pick a topic and genre (plus platform, and later engine and target audience). Then run three production stages, each splitting time across three focus areas with sliders. Stage 1 is Engine, Gameplay and Story/Quests. Stage 2 is Dialogues, Level Design and AI. Stage 3 is World Design, Graphics and Sound. Success depends on matching the slider focus to the genre's weightings.

- Confidence: high (3-0 across 5 merged claims)
- Evidence: Unanimous votes; several reviews agree, and so do the community wiki and guides that the verifiers cited. The sources are blogs or reviews, but the mechanic is basic and has been stable since 2012.
- Sources: https://destructoid.com/?p=150087, https://gamechronicles.com/game-dev-tycoon-review-pcsteam, https://miltonlane.substack.com/p/game-dev-tycoon

### In GDT, scoring has two currencies, Design and Technology points. Each focus area has a hidden ratio of how much design versus tech skill feeds it (Engine is roughly 80% tech, 20% design). The review compares the Design/Tech balance with the genre's ideal, and also weighs topic/genre fit and bugs. There are no separate gameplay, graphics or sound scores (that category model belongs to Game Dev Story).

- Confidence: medium (3-0 (2 claims merged))
- Evidence: Blog sources, backed up by the verifiers' knowledge of the wiki. The 80/20 figure is an illustration, not a verified constant. A more specific claim that the score is a product of factors was refuted 1-2.
- Sources: https://dshen6.github.io/posts/clarity-in-game-dev-tycoon, https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/?p=49078

### In GDT, review scores are relative. Quality is benchmarked against the company's previous top score: the target can range from 1% to 120% of that top score. A hit therefore raises the bar, and a later game of the same quality reviews worse. The game never discloses this rule.

- Confidence: medium (3-0 (2 claims merged))
- Evidence: Two blogs, one of them a low-quality scraped blog, matching what the community found by reading the game's code. Verifiers noted that absolute quality is still the input; the reviews show that quality measured against a moving bar.
- Sources: https://brownobse1959.blogspot.com/2021/11/game-dev-tycoon-tech-design-balance.html, https://dshen6.github.io/posts/clarity-in-game-dev-tycoon

### The most consistently documented weakness is opaque scoring and feedback. Reviews give little insight and do not address the player's own design choices. The score reveal 'randomly spun through numbers', which made choices feel meaningless. The best play depends on tedious external bookkeeping. The in-game post-release reports soften this only partly, and they unlock with progression.

- Confidence: medium (3-0 on 4 claims, 2-1 on 1)
- Evidence: Several independent critics and players, all opinion pieces. Stretching this from GDT to the whole genre is the sources' own generalisation. Related claims that success is just luck or a solved formula were refuted.
- Sources: https://destructoid.com/?p=150087, https://www.destructoid.com/reviews/review-game-dev-tycoon/, https://dshen6.github.io/posts/clarity-in-game-dev-tycoon, https://mechanicsofmagic.com/?p=47877

### Roguelike tycoon precedents exist, but none found is a game-dev tycoon. GOD CANDLE is a roguelite trading game that describes itself with 'The market is the dungeon, and it's never the same twice'. Gamestonk Simulator: Gone Rogue (a retro game shop tycoon) has a permadeath Survival Mode in which collected Retros carry over to buy permanent power-ups, alongside an optional Tycoon Mode with no game over. Goblin Vyke pairs procedural dungeon heists at night with a shop-selling loop by day.

- Confidence: medium (3-0 except the meta-progression claim (2-1))
- Evidence: Store pages and aggregators: developer marketing, adequate only to show the games exist and how they are framed. GOD CANDLE's fail state (liquidation, leverage) was refuted 0-3 as stated. Gamestonk's permadeath is soft, because meta-currency persists between runs.
- Sources: https://nodal.gg/game/god-candle-5002930, https://store.steampowered.com/app/3349970/Gamestonk_Simulator:_Gone_Rogue/, https://vaporlens.app/app/3794610/goblin_vyke_the_thief_tycoon.md

## Caveats

The coverage is narrow. Almost every surviving claim concerns Game Dev Tycoon. Nothing survived on Mad Games Tycoon 1 and 2, Game Dev Story, Software Inc., Startup Company or Indie Dev Story. Nothing survived on staff stats, traits, training or burnout, on hype or bug formulas, on market lifecycles or eras, or on late-game snowballing. Most sources are blogs or launch-era reviews from 2013 and 2014, and some verifiers relied on prior knowledge rather than live checks. The formula details (the 1%-120% band, the 80/20 ratios) come from community reverse-engineering of particular game versions. The precedent games come from marketing copy. The criticism of passive, repetitive play was refuted, so repetitiveness is not established by this evidence.

## Open questions

- What exactly are the formulas for hype, bugs and sales decay in GDT, and how do Mad Games Tycoon 2 and Software Inc. compute review scores?
- How do the other titles model staff (traits, burnout, training), and which of those systems do players praise or criticise?
- How do the market lifecycles, platform eras and trend systems in Mad Games Tycoon 2 and Software Inc. work, and how quickly do players solve them or snowball through them?
- Could a self-relative review bar like GDT's work as a readable difficulty ramp in a run where cash is health and bankruptcy is permadeath, if it were shown openly rather than hidden?

## Refuted claims

- Success comes across as either luck or a solved formula, not readable strategy. (1-2) https://destructoid.com/?p=150087
- Liquidation is the fail state that ends a run, which works like permadeath, and leverage goes up to 10x. (0-3) https://nodal.gg/game/god-candle-5002930
- Debt repayment is a core pressure system, and players cite debt pressure as a criticism (weight 0.21). (0-3) https://vaporlens.app/app/3794610/goblin_vyke_the_thief_tycoon.md
- Game Dev Tycoon (v1.5.24) game score is a product of Design+Tech, quality, platform-genre fit, topic-audience fit, bug ratio and trend factor. (1-2) https://brownobse1959.blogspot.com/2021/11/game-dev-tycoon-tech-design-balance.html
- Success in Game Dev Tycoon comes from trial and error or from exploiting a known formula, not from informed decisions. (0-3) https://www.destructoid.com/reviews/review-game-dev-tycoon/
- Early playthroughs are opaque: players can put resources into changes that barely affect success. (0-3) https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/?p=49078
- Unorthodox genre mixing is punished, and success is measured only by money and review scores. (1-2) https://www.digitallydownloaded.net/?p=49078
- The core loop is largely passive and repetitive, best suited to short sessions. (0-3) https://gamechronicles.com/game-dev-tycoon-review-pcsteam
